# Founder Story Interviewer v1.0 Product Specification

## Workbench

Founder Story Studio

## Domain table

Interview/quote bank

## Metrics

- Story Completeness
- Consent Safety
- Quote Quality

## Domain rows

- Interview script generated
- Consent recorded
- Origin story captured
- Community mission captured
- Quote bank tagged
- Sensitive topics flagged
- About copy generated
- Approved quote sheet exported

## Generated deliverables

- Founder bio
- About page copy
- Social story pack

## Validation gates

- Quotes require consent
- Usage channels approved
- Sensitive topics flagged before export
